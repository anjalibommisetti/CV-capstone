❓ Frequently Asked Viva Questions & Standard Answers

Q1: Why do we use Gaussian Blur before segmentation and classification?

Answer: Digital camera sensors introduce high-frequency noise (such as salt-and-pepper noise and ISO grain). Gaussian filtering applies a 2D Gaussian bell-shaped distribution kernel to average out high-frequency variations while preserving prominent leaf edges and necrotic lesion boundaries.

Q2: Why use HSV color space instead of RGB for leaf segmentation?

Answer: RGB combines color and illumination into every channel, making it extremely vulnerable to shadows and uneven sunlight. HSV decouples chromaticity (Hue) and color saturation (Saturation) from brightness (Value). Plant chlorophyll pigments remain consistently detectable in Hue (25–90) regardless of shadows.

Q3: What is MobileNetV2 and why is it preferred over heavy networks like VGG16?

Answer: MobileNetV2 uses Depthwise Separable Convolutions and Inverted Residual Blocks with linear bottlenecks. This drastically reduces parameter count (3.5M parameters compared to VGG16's 138M) and computation costs (FLOPs), allowing real-time inference on standard laptops and agricultural edge devices without losing accuracy.

Q4: What is GLCM and what features does it extract?

Answer: The Gray-Level Co-occurrence Matrix (GLCM) tabulates how often pairs of pixels with specific values and in a specified spatial relationship occur in an image. It extracts second-order statistical texture properties: Contrast (surface roughness), Homogeneity (texture uniformity), Energy (textural order), and Correlation.

Q5: Where do real model performance numbers appear?

Answer: Genuine metrics are generated directly upon running python train.py. They are saved inside the reports/ directory:
• reports/classification_report_mobilenetv2.txt (Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1)
• reports/confusion_matrix_mobilenetv2.png (Per-class true vs predicted matrix)
• reports/training_curves_mobilenet.png (Accuracy and Loss plots over epochs)
• reports/model_comparison.json (Direct Custom CNN vs MobileNetV2 comparison)
