✨ Computer Vision & Deep Learning Workflow

End-to-end multi-stage pipeline combining classical OpenCV digital image processing with deep transfer learning:

Stage 01

📸 Smart Ingestion

Validates image dimensions, filters camera sensor noise via 5×5 Gaussian Blur, and normalizes pixels into tensor bounds. Intercepts human faces & non-plant objects.

Gaussian Filter Haar Cascades 224×224
Stage 02

🔬 HSV Segmentation

Isolates chlorophyll tissue from arbitrary backgrounds. Isolates necrotic lesions and calculates GLCM texture moments & shape compactness.

HSV Masking Lesion Overlay GLCM Texture
